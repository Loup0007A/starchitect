# Changelog

## 1.1.0

- **All-new look**: aged newspaper paper, a big masthead with ornaments, a drop cap, a pull quote in the middle of the article, animated stars, and a **seal stamped on the page** (gold rosette with 1 to 3 stars, red ink stamp for no star, a dirt rosette for dirt huts). Score bars fill up when the paper opens.
- **Press photo**: when the critic arrives, a photo of your build is taken (camera flash and shutter), developed in sepia with film grain, and printed in the article. Photos also appear as thumbnails in the Guide.
- **The critic's advice**: every review ends with up to 3 concrete tips ("add about 6 more windows", "light up the 9 pitch-dark spots"…). The **Show me** button (or `/starchitect show`) marks every pitch-dark spot of the build with red particles for 30 seconds.
- **Building gauge** (key **G** or `/starchitect gauge`): a live estimate of the score of the build around you, with the 8 criteria and its weak spot, updated as you build.
- New visit banner (paper strip, sliding in, magnifying glass, shimmering progress bar) and a redesigned Guide (photo cards, seals, progress to the next title).
- Nouveau look complet (papier vieilli, lettrine, sceaux animés), photo de presse en sépia, conseils du critique avec bouton « Montrer », mètre de chantier (touche G).

## 1.0.0

- First release: a snobbish architecture critic reviews your builds (palette, depth, lighting, windows, roof, symmetry, interior, garden) and publishes the review in the newspaper, with 0 to 3 stars.
- 4 critics with their own tastes, surprise visits, return visits, the Starchitect Guide with ranks, English and French.
- Forge 1.21.1, NeoForge 1.21.1 and Forge 1.20.1. 100% client-side.
- Première version : un critique d'architecture snob note vos constructions de 0 à 3 étoiles. Quatre critiques, visites surprises, guide, français et anglais.
